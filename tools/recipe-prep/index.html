STEP 01

What are you baking?

One ingredient per line. Use g (or a Korean gram spelling). Numbers such as 1,200g and 2.5g are accepted.

All confirmed ingredients use the same pan ratio. Enter dough or batter ingredients only; calculate icing and decorations separately.

✳

Before calculating,
check each ingredient.

You can edit the parsed names and grams. Lines the tool cannot read stay visible too.

Cups, ml and egg counts are not converted to weight. Enter grams yourself or exclude the line.

The example uses demonstration numbers. It is not a tested baking recipe.

Review parsed ingredients

Correct any values, then confirm. Excluded lines will not appear in the checklist.
