Calculation method and limits

Scale ingredients by
the ratio of base areas.

Enter inside pan dimensions in cm. The calculation assumes the same batter depth in the original and target pans.

The formulas

  • Round area = π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × number of pans
  • Rectangular area = width × length × number of pans
  • Ratio = total target pan area ÷ total original pan area
  • Target ingredient weight = original ingredient weight × ratio

Pan counts must be whole numbers of at least 1. Dimensions and ingredient weights must be greater than 0. Values outside the calculation range produce an error.

Try the example step by step

  1. Open the checklist and choose “Try an example”. This fills six ingredient lines and one 18 cm round pan → one 21 cm round pan.
  2. Compare the parsed ingredient names and grams with the source, then choose “Confirm ingredients”.
  3. Check the inside diameters and pan counts, then choose “Create a checklist”. The displayed ratio is 1.361×; 120 g of cake flour becomes 163.333 g, and 2.5 g of baking powder becomes 3.403 g.
  4. Check off ingredients as you weigh them. Choose “Save in this browser” to resume later. Save again to keep any subsequent input or checkbox changes.

This example tests the tool's features. It is not a verified baking recipe, and the displayed decimal places do not indicate your scale's accuracy.

Supported ingredient input

All confirmed ingredients use the same pan ratio. Enter dough or batter ingredients only; calculate icing and decorations separately.

Write one ingredient and one gram amount per line, such as “Cake flour 120g”, “120 g Unsalted butter”, “Milk 2.5g” or “Flour 1,200g”. The unit g and the Korean gram spellings are accepted.

Cups, ml, counts, fractions, ranges, kg and multiple ingredients on one line are not automatically converted. Ambiguous input, including ingredient names with numbers, needs review. Unread lines stay visible for you to correct or exclude.

Input is limited to 500 lines and 100,000 characters, ingredient names to 200 characters, and numeric fields to 40 characters. You must review names and weights after parsing before calculating.

How to review unread lines

“Eggs 2” and “Milk 100ml” are not automatically converted to grams. Use a weight provided by the original recipe or one you measured, then edit “Ingredient name” and “Grams (g)” in that row. Do not guess grams from a count or volume alone.

Selecting “Exclude this line” removes that ingredient from the calculation and checklist. Excluding a required ingredient just because you cannot weigh it leaves the checklist incomplete. Compare it with the original recipe again before calculating.

Changing pans or ingredients clears the previous checklist. After editing ingredients, choose “Confirm ingredients” again, then “Create a checklist” for a new result.

Precision and real baking

Calculations use JavaScript floating-point numbers. Displayed values are rounded to three decimal places; values below 0.001 g use scientific notation. Displayed digits do not represent actual weighing accuracy.

Only simple round or rectangular pans with straight sides are assumed. Center holes, slopes, decorative shapes, pan height and capacity are not included. Scaling ingredients does not guarantee unchanged baking time, temperature or batter behavior.

Check the pan's capacity and your scale's precision separately. Follow tested recipes and manufacturer instructions for baking time, temperature and food safety.
