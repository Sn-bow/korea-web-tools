About

A small experiment in
getting ready to weigh.

Paste and review a gram-based recipe, scale ingredients by pan area, and use a weighing checklist in English or Korean.

Available now

  • Conservative parsing of gram-based ingredients, with a manual review and correction step
  • Area ratios for round or rectangular pans, including the number of pans
  • A weighing checklist, printing and browser storage only when you press Save

No external libraries, accounts, server-side calculations, ads, external analytics services or tracking scripts are included. Ingredient inputs are not sent elsewhere. The server keeps limited page request counts, as described in Privacy.

What we cannot claim

Automated calculation and state tests do not establish baking results, real-world usability or compatibility with every browser. The tool has not been verified on every device or assistive technology.

We do not claim professional baking review or guarantee revenue or search visibility. Check your inputs and follow a tested recipe and the pan manufacturer's instructions.

Public feedback

Report bugs or suggest improvements through public GitHub issues. A GitHub account is required, and anyone can read what you post. Do not post personal information, passwords, private recipes or server logs. No separate private operator contact address is currently listed.

You do not need to enter or send personal information to use this tool. For storage and server error logs, read Privacy.
