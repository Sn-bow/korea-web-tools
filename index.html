What can you do here?

  1. Paste the ingredients from a gram-based recipe you already have.
  2. Review the names and amounts. Correct or exclude any lines the tool could not read.
  3. Enter the inside dimensions and number of original and target pans.
  4. Weigh and check off the scaled ingredients, or print your checklist.

Use the Save button to keep one checklist in this browser. There is no account or server storage.

Limits to know before calculating

The tool compares base areas of simple round or rectangular pans with straight sides. It does not account for pan height, capacity, center holes, sloped sides or decorative shapes.

All confirmed ingredients use the same pan ratio. Enter dough or batter ingredients only; calculate icing and decorations separately.

Cups, ml and egg counts are not automatically converted to grams. The tool does not assess baking time, temperature, overflow or food safety.

This is an early experiment. Check the results against your original recipe and use the precision your scale can actually measure.
